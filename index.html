HOW IT WORKS

Quick start

  1. New student? Select Create an account and register your name, roll number, department, section, and a password of at least 12 characters.
  2. Your account signs in automatically after registration. When you return, sign in with the same roll number and password.
  3. Check that your registered details are shown, then accept the public leaderboard notice before playing. You enter your details during sign-up; they are not entered again after sign-in.
  4. Pick an unlocked level and review its topics. Level 1 is Pseudocode Practice.
  5. Answer within 60 seconds per question. Wrong answers and timeouts use a life; correct answers earn points.
  6. Pass a level to unlock the next one and save your progress.
